3.1 Learning Layer: Prefixes Extraction

Learning Pipeline:

1

Prefixes Extraction

Process

  • Extract sequential prefixes from process traces
  • Generate input-output pairs (X, y)

Key Concepts

  • Prefix (X): Sequence of events up to a point
  • Target (y): Next activity, resource, or termination
  • Termination symbol (⊥): End of process
